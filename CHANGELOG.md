## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-13

- Initial package: `Names::first()`, `last()`, `middle()`, `full()` and
  `person()` over the bundled `en` and `ru` datasets, the `PersonName` value
  object with `full()`/`initialLast()`/`lastInitials()`, and the `Gender` enum.
